# Retro — Sprint 1 — 2026-08-27

## What we set out to do
Run a 2-agent pilot of the Field-Report Assistant for one week and get
ops-lead review time down from ~45 minutes to under 5.

## What actually happened
Both agents used it all week. Review time dropped to ~8 minutes (from 45) —
progress, but short of the 5-minute target. One report needed a manual date
fix.

## What we learned
Relative date phrasing in notes ("last Tuesday") confuses the formatter —
better to fix it at the input side (agent writes absolute dates) than to
make the assistant guess. Ops lead trusts the output more each week, which
is most of the remaining time-to-target gap, not a tooling problem.

## What changes next sprint
Update field-note guidance to require absolute dates. Extend the pilot to
3 more agents rather than declaring it done at 5 minutes exactly — one more
week of data first. Mandate and Contract stay as written; no changes.
