The enterprise AI charter

Adoption is easy to start. Getting it wrong is easy to do quietly.

Where we probably stand today

No company-wide plan, no admin console, no written policy — normal for a startup. People already using AI (some of you, on Claude) are doing it on personal accounts, so whatever that vendor's consumer defaults are already apply, with zero company visibility.

Two toggles to find and turn off

Model training on your content — often on by default.

Indefinite retention — often on by default.

These are settings, not personality traits of the tool.

Quotas are real

Heavy users learn fast: chat subscriptions come with usage quotas that a normal workday can burn through.

"Zero data retention" — read the fine print

True ZDR is typically an API-tier arrangement — costs more, and is not automatically granted. It often exists "on paper" more than as a default every customer gets. Confirm it's actually contracted — don't assume it from a sales page.

What to do this week

A one-page interim guideline beats no guideline: no customer/ financial data on personal AI accounts, starting today. Move to a company-owned plan next.

Scoping a project

How much freedom to give the agent

The rule of thumb

The narrower the scope, the more freedom you can safely give the agent — and the more effective it is.

Step by step

Start narrow, human reviews everything → prove it out → widen the leash deliberately, not all at once.

Staying on track

The real risk: losing the goal along the way

Long conversations drift

Even compacted/summarized ones — a conversation that keeps answering "what's the natural next question" will happily walk you down a rabbit hole with nothing to do with your original goal.

The fix is a cadence, not more care

You can't out-focus drift mid-conversation. Force a regular checkpoint: reread the mandate, ask one blunt question — are we still on track?

ATLAS

Running an AI-assisted project, not just a conversation

Mandate → Contract → State → Sprint

  • Mandate — the fixed goal
  • Contract — what it must never do, and what it commits to
  • State — progress, learnings, backlog, live
  • Sprint — optional, can just be a live session
  • Retro — the checkpoint that catches drift

What it stops

Scope wandering toward the last convenient conversation. Connectors attached with no one asking what they expose. Every session re-deriving context from zero. Drift that never gets corrected because there was never a checkpoint.
