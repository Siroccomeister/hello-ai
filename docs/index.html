Content is now a paid, licensed input

Not scraped for free anymore — AI companies increasingly pay publishers directly for the right to use their content, either to train on or as a live feed the AI can cite.

OpenAIAxios, The Guardian, The Washington Post, Schibsted
GoogleAssociated Press — real-time AP content feeds into Gemini
MetaCNN, Fox News, USA Today — for Llama
AmazonThe New York Times, Condé Nast, Hearst — for Rufus

Why this matters here: the AI companies themselves treat sourcing and references as worth paying for. Same discipline applies when you use AI — check sources, verify claims, don't take an unreferenced answer at face value.

MCP — Model Context Protocol

The ready-made Claude.ai connectors are one version of this. MCP is the open standard underneath — for connecting to any system, not just pre-built ones.

GenericDrive, Gmail, Calendar — click to authorize
Custom (MCP)Your own systems — built once, reusable

Same rule either way: scope is whatever the credential can reach.
