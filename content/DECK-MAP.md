## The through-line, in one breath
Africa has leapfrogged before — mobile money past bank branches, mobile
phones past landlines. AI is the next one, and it's already happening
whether or not HT prepares for it (the numbers in Chapter 2 say so). So:
here's how to use it at whatever level you're at (3), here's the one rule
that keeps it safe (4), here's what the company needs to set up now, while
it's small enough to do properly (5), here's how to run a real project
with it without losing the thread (6–7), and here's the one thing to do
before you leave the room (8).

## Tone notes for delivery
Serious about the risk (privacy, drift) without being cautious about the
opportunity — the leapfrog framing is the enthusiasm, not forced hype.
Encouraging: HT being an early-stage company with no formal AI setup yet is
framed as an advantage (nothing to unwind), not a shortcoming.
