# Opening — why this matters now

This is the hook, before the tiers. The point: this isn't a niche
productivity tool, it's a real behavior shift happening at population
scale, right now — and it cuts both ways (disrupts incumbents, creates real
enterprise opportunity if you're prepared for it).

## The behavior shift
Most people in the room already do this without naming it: instead of
searching and clicking through links, they ask an assistant directly and
read the answer. That single habit change is why ad-funded search and
social platforms are exposed — the click, and the time spent on their
surface, is the thing their business model depends on, and it's the thing
this shift removes.

## Three hard facts to underpin the message

1. **Publisher referral traffic from Google is down roughly a third
   globally year-on-year (38% in the US)**, as AI-generated answers
   increasingly satisfy the search itself — the "zero-click search" effect.
   This is the ad-model disruption made concrete: fewer clicks reaching the
   sites the ad economy was built on.
   — [Press Gazette, "Global publisher Google traffic dropped by a third in
   2025"](https://pressgazette.co.uk/media-audience-and-business-data/google-traffic-down-2025-trends-report-2026/)

2. **ChatGPT alone went from ~400M to ~900M weekly active users in about a
   year (early 2025 → February 2026), nearing 1B weekly by mid-2026.** This
   is not early-adopter usage anymore — it's mainstream scale, reached
   faster than any consumer app before it.
   — [Reuters / OpenAI reporting via industry roundups, Aug
   2026](https://www.demandsage.com/chatgpt-statistics/)

3. **Publishers are now explicitly selling their content as AI training
   data** — licensing deals with AI companies went from 0 in 2022, to 28 in
   2024, to a projected 36+ in 2026 (OpenAI alone has signed with Axios, The
   Guardian, The Washington Post, and others; Google's first was with the
   Associated Press). Content itself has become a licensed input, not just
   something search indexes for free.
   — [Digiday, "A timeline of the major deals between publishers and AI
   tech companies"](https://digiday.com/media/a-timeline-of-the-major-deals-between-publishers-and-ai-tech-companies-in-2025/)

## The turn: disruption *and* opportunity
Same shift, two sides. It's a real threat to ad-funded incumbents (Google,
Meta) because attention and clicks are moving off their surfaces. But for
an enterprise like HT, the same shift is a genuine capability upgrade —
*if* the organization prepares for it rather than adopting it ad hoc. That
preparation is the rest of this session: what tier of tool for what task,
what the enterprise needs to have in place first (the AI charter,
`content/05-enterprise-charter/`), and how to run real projects with it
without losing the goal along the way (`content/07-drift-and-retros/`).
