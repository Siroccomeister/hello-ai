# Tier 2 — Builder AI (Projects, artifacts, connectors)

Audience: staff who want AI to do more than answer one-off questions — build
a small recurring workflow, a working document, a connected assistant.

## Talking points
- **Projects:** a persistent workspace with its own instructions and files —
  use this instead of re-pasting the same background every conversation.
  Good for: a recurring report format, a team's onboarding assistant, a
  living FAQ.
- **Artifacts:** when the output is itself a thing (a one-pager, a small
  tool, a chart, a mini dashboard) rather than just an answer — it becomes
  an editable, shareable page instead of a wall of chat text.
- **Connectors (Drive, Gmail, calendars, etc.):** this is where "builder"
  tier crosses into needing the privacy rules — a connector doesn't hand
  over one file, it hands over everything the connected account can reach.
  **This is the single most important thing to land with this audience.**
- **When to go this route vs. stay at Tier 1:** if you're doing the same
  kind of request more than a couple of times, or the output needs to be a
  polished shareable thing, it's worth the setup cost of a Project.

## Live demo idea
Build a small Project live (e.g., a "field report formatter" with a fixed
template as project instructions) and show a connector's access scope
before connecting it.

## Privacy note for this tier
Before connecting any data source: know what's behind it, who owns it, and
whether this is the right place for it to be visible. See
`content/04-privacy/` and `BOUNDARIES.md`.
